The App is an interactive educational simulation designed to illustrate vector flow fields, phase portraits, dynamical systems, and chaotic strange attractors (such as the Lorenz, Rössler, and custom dynamical attractors):

3. Mathematical & Numerical Approximation Notice

All particle trajectories, vector field integrations, and attractor phase plots are calculated using finite-difference numerical methods (e.g. Runge-Kutta / Euler integration) subject to finite floating-point precision:

  • Chaotic systems are inherently sensitive to initial conditions. Numerical simulations may diverge or exhibit numerical instability under extreme parameter combinations.

  • No representations are made regarding the quantitative precision or real-world physical fidelity of the simulated trajectories.
