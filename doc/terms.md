## 2. Educational & Demonstration Purpose Only

The App is an interactive educational simulation designed to illustrate vector
flow fields, phase portraits, dynamical systems, and chaotic strange
attractors (such as the Lorenz, Rössler, and custom dynamical attractors):

* The App is intended solely for personal, academic, and exploratory learning.

* It is not certified or designed for professional fluid dynamics engineering,
  aerospace modeling, weather forecasting, structural analysis, or
  mission-critical simulation.

---

## 3. Mathematical & Numerical Approximation Notice

All particle trajectories, vector field integrations, and attractor phase
plots are calculated using finite-difference numerical methods (e.g.
Runge-Kutta / Euler integration) subject to finite floating-point precision:

* Chaotic systems are inherently sensitive to initial conditions. Numerical
  simulations may diverge or exhibit numerical instability under extreme
  parameter combinations.

* No representations are made regarding the quantitative precision or
  real-world physical fidelity of the simulated trajectories.

## 4. Visual & Motion Sensitivity Notice

The App displays complex moving particles, flow lines, and rapid phase space
rotations:

* If you experience motion sickness, dizziness, eyestrain, or visual
  disorientation while viewing dense animated trajectories, please pause the
  animation or reduce particle density and speed.
